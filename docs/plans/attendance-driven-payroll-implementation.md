### Form 2316 field audit (2026-10-08)

The official BIR September 2021 ENCS Form 2316 requires employee TIN and RDO, registered and local addresses with separate ZIP codes, employee birth date/contact, statutory minimum-wage rates where applicable, present- and previous-employer identities, compensation totals by the form's non-taxable/regular/supplementary categories, withholding totals, and employer/employee declarations and signatures. See the [official BIR form](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/2316%20Sep%202021%20ENCS_Final_corrected.pdf).

The data model now stores a tax-year-scoped employee TIN/RDO and separate registered/local addresses, plus previous-employer TIN/name/address/ZIP and covered dates. These fields have their own approval flag/source and completeness validation, separate from approval of the payroll tax totals. The existing employee record and 201-file annex remain unchanged. However, prior-employer declarations still do not provide itemized compensation categories, and current payroll snapshots do not classify all Form 2316 lines (for example representation, transportation, COLA, housing, commissions, fees, hazard, PERA credit, and prior-employer withholding) with certificate-level provenance. Therefore no generated or emailed Form 2316 is supported yet. Keep termination/year-end certificate blockers and payroll finalization/delivery gates in place; do not infer missing lines as zero or copy one address into both address sections.

Before enabling certificate generation, retain source-backed classifications for every Form 2316 compensation line; reconcile generated totals to finalized payroll and opening declarations; then render and independently compare a sample PDF against the official form. Support multiple previous employers or block that case explicitly. Signatures and substituted-filing declarations must remain unasserted until the applicable human attestation is captured. This audit is a data-contract gap assessment, not an approval of the form version or tax treatment.

## Scope clarification and statutory follow-up (2026-10-07)

The first supported employee profile is ordinary local Philippine private-sector employment. Government, foreign/non-resident, self-employed/voluntary, household-worker and special-tax profiles are outside this first scope. Minimum-wage-earner status and unusual taxable/non-taxable compensation still require explicit employee data and rules; they must not be inferred from salary.

The confirmed collection policy is one employee and employer contribution collection per statutory type per calendar month, assigned to the last payroll run in that month. Policy confirmation now requires `frequency: once_monthly` and `collection_period: last_period`; the database ledger, reasoned correction linkage and final-period-only finalization guard are implemented. With resolved full-month attendance, ordinary monthly/daily/hourly drafts calculate SSS and Pag-IBIG from actual regular/approved-overtime earnings and PhilHealth from fixed basic salary. Daily/hourly PhilHealth monthly equivalent uses the confirmed monthly divisor, effective shift duration and effective salary periods; it deliberately ignores absences and overtime. Integration coverage checks daily/hourly payroll inputs for the final semi-monthly run. Opening tax-year declarations plus gapless finalized-period snapshots support BIR annualization. Minimum-wage-earner and cumulative-average tax cases remain blocked.

Review of [SSS's official employer/employee contribution schedule](https://www.sss.gov.ph/sss-contribution-table/) and [Circular 2024-006](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/CI-2024-006-Publication.pdf) confirms that employer bands map monthly compensation to an MSC; employee and employer MPF shares are separate above ₱20,000 MSC, and EC is employer-paid. The earlier model selected flat amounts by MSC ranges, so it could not represent the official compensation mapping accurately. The new schema separates compensation bands from the mapped MSC, calculates from compensation, and fails closed on legacy rows without the new mapping. Migration regression tests check the 61 seeded bands, representative boundaries, MSC mapping and shares against the circular. This verifies seeded 2025 values, but not later schedule amendments or employer approval.

Review of [HDMF Circular 460 as summarized by the Philippine Information Agency from a Pag-IBIG official](https://pia.gov.ph/news/employers-who-fail-to-remit-p200-pag-ibig-share-to-be-penalized/) confirmed the employee rates are 1% at or below ₱1,500 and 2% above it, the employer rate is 2%, and the contribution salary cap increased to ₱10,000 effective February 2024. The old Pag-IBIG selector also used only the first row of an effective schedule; existing calculator fixtures had the employee rates reversed and outdated expectations. The selector, fixtures and amount assertions are now corrected, with boundary and cap regressions. These tests validate the configured band mechanics; production schedule rows still require authorized source review before activation.

The [PhilHealth 2025-0002 advisory](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2025/PA2025-0002.pdf) confirms the 2025 direct-contributor rate, floor and ceiling and says to use fixed monthly basic salary without reducing it for absences or unpaid leave.

# Payroll Calculation Comparison

Use this worksheet for the human acceptance comparison after the final test gate. It is a calculation review, not a request to approve statutory settings or enable production finalization.

## Keep inputs identical

Start with the documented fictional case: ordinary local private-sector employee, non-MWE, ₱26,000 monthly basic salary, 22-workday divisor, 8-hour weekday shift, twice-monthly group with October 1–15 as the first period, 11 scheduled workdays, one unpaid absence, no overtime, no leave, holiday, allowance, loan, or adjustment. Compare gross regular pay and the absence deduction first; add one variable at a time afterward. Use a separate full-month/final-period case for the once-monthly statutory deductions.

Record the exact effective date, pay-period type, salary basis, attendance minutes, company policy version, statutory table version, and rounding rule for every comparison. A difference without matching inputs is not actionable.

## Comparison cautions

- The online BIR withholding calculator currently displays older de minimis limits. Do not use those limits as the current result; use the applicable law and table version instead.
- PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0042 is addressed to OFWs. It reaffirms the 5% rate effective January 2025; for employed-member MBS treatment, use the employer advisory and contribution circular above. The rate schedule remains company-maintained and must be rechecked before use for a later effective year.
- SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG schedules in the app are versioned company records, not live agency feeds. Check the effective schedule and source before treating its result as correct.
- Use only fictional employee data. Record a discrepancy, input set, source/version, and expected result; never resolve a financial difference by silently changing a live employee record.

Finalization and real payslip email remain disabled until the owner reviews and accepts supported sample calculations and the remaining operational checks pass.
